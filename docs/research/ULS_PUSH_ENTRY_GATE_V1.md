# ULS + PUSH v7 — frozen entry-gating feasibility study

**Status:** COMPLETE — frozen paired VALIDATION criterion passed; no OOS or
LOCKBOX access.

**Date:** 2026-08-26

## Question

Can a model trained on all unique recorded TP800/SL800 TRAIN trades improve the
selected fixed ULS + PUSH configuration on the already-defined VALIDATION
dataset by deciding when to abstain from entry?

This is an entry gate only. TP and SL remain fixed at 800/800. The paired fixed
baseline is source child 344, TRAIN run `01M0ZECR10K8XERRYZYK3ZRPAT`, because it
had the largest searched aggregate provisional net PnL. That selection is a
searched TRAIN maximum and not independent evidence.

## Frozen evidence design

- Sources: exactly the 200 TP800/SL800 children (ordinals 200–399) of sweep
  `uls-push-v7-train-owner-200x4-brackets-v1`.
- Raw inventory established before fitting: 961,609 emitted events and 405,150
  executed trades across 61,624 repeated market episodes.
- Exclusion: 344 boundary-forced closes. Expected retained corpus: 404,806
  rows and 61,576 unique market episodes.
- Duplicate control: no bracket copies; exact samples refused; identical
  event-time episodes group-split and inverse-frequency weighted.
- Target: recorded provisional net PnL, permanently unverified. Missing
  counterfactual trades are not fabricated.
- Fit/calibration/internal-test: chronological 70/15/15 with exit-boundary
  purge, as specified in ADR-0026.
- Model: deterministic 64-tree regression forest and one cutoff selected only
  from the calibration span for deployment child 344.

The context-carrier config is
`config/experiments/uls-push-entry-context-carrier-train-v1.json`, generated by
`scripts/generate_uls_entry_carrier.py`. The immutable corpus and fitted state
will live under `<runs_root>/training-bundles/uls-entry-selector-v1-*`.

## One-shot VALIDATION comparison

The freeze tool `trading_research.ops.phase8_entry_validation` will generate
one immutable two-child sweep after the TRAIN bundle exists. Both children use
`XAUUSD_S_M1_VALIDATION_V1`, identical bounds, child-344 ULS parameters, the
same frozen forest, TP800/SL800 and the same unverified cost binding. They differ
only in the chosen generic output:

| Child | Output | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `model_gate` | `score` | signed event only when predicted value exceeds the frozen cutoff |
| `fixed_best_baseline` | `value_0` | every original signed event, ignoring the prediction |

Run the two children concurrently. Primary success is higher provisional net
PnL for `model_gate` with at least 30 trades. Also report gross PnL, provisional
cost, trade count, win rate, maximum drawdown and exact differences. Results
must be indexed so both ordinary runs appear in Explorer.

No result may change the corpus, forest, cutoff, source configuration or
success rule after VALIDATION is opened. OOS and LOCKBOX are outside this study.

## Mandatory interpretation limits

The broker/account price and cost binding is unverified; slippage, financing,
latency and rejection are absent. Labels cover executed trades only and many
source trades exited on a later target change, making them dependent on their
source signal path. This experiment is a feasibility comparison, not verified
profitability evidence.

## Frozen result

The immutable TRAIN bundle is
`uls-entry-selector-v1-cdb9bd758cdd9d3c48df`, built at clean commit
`ea467513f2f4f9bf149463179c5821173759912a`. It retained the preregistered
404,806 rows and selected cutoff `-0.5440342564262345`. On the deployment
configuration's internal TRAIN test span, the gate selected 170 of 275 trades
and recorded provisional net PnL 102.37 versus 122.94 for the unscreened
baseline. That internal warning was accepted without any retuning before the
one-shot VALIDATION comparison.

Both canonical VALIDATION children completed successfully:

| Arm | Run | Trades | Gross PnL | Provisional cost | Provisional net PnL | Win rate | Max drawdown |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Fixed best baseline | `01M0ZTKDZCWWFYG9SVRC0HEHSK` | 701 | 102.26 | 182.26 | -80.00 | 44.6505% | 176.40 |

The model-minus-baseline difference was +63.51 gross, -63.96 provisional
cost, +127.47 provisional net PnL, -246 trades, +3.4814 percentage points in
win rate and -66.93 maximum drawdown. The model retained more than the frozen
minimum of 30 trades and had higher provisional net PnL, so the preregistered
primary criterion passed.
